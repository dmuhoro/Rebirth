# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the spirit of Christian charity, humility, and mutual edification, we as contributors and maintainers of **Rebirth** pledge to make participation in our project and our community a respectful, constructive, and spiritually edifying experience for everyone, regardless of background, technical experience, or tradition.

## Guiding Principles

As Colossians 3:12–14 encourages:
> *"Put on then, as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved, compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience, bearing with one another and, if one has a complaint against another, forgiving each other; as the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive. And above all these put on love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony."*

We commit to:
- Demonstrating grace, empathy, and patience in discussions and code reviews.
- Engaging in theological dialogue with charity, intellectual honesty, and mutual respect.
- Welcoming newcomers and patiently answering questions.
- Focusing on what is best for the disciples and seekers who use this devotional tool.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Sectarian hostility or uncharitable dismissiveness toward other brothers and sisters in Christ.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative Christian community.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
