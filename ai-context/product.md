# ai-context/product.md — Product Agent

Authority: Overrides all on scope (prevents overbuilding).

## Vision
Meet God in the Word every day, in the state you are actually in. Contemplative, audio-first devotional with original-language study and an AI reflection partner that always points back to Scripture.

## In scope (v1)
Contemplative audio lessons, exegetical Scripture studies (Hebrew/Greek), emotion-aware journaling + AI pastoral counseling (3-pillar bounded), Daily Contemplation, bookmarking/favorites/export, PWA offline + lock-screen controls, secure rate-limited Gemini proxy, Convex sync + guest mode, mobile-first WCAG AA, Formation Journeys (6 figures + Christ capstone).

## Out of scope (v1)
Social feeds/public journals, church replacement, prophecy/"word from God", manifestation tool, crisis counseling/medical diagnosis, gamified spiritual scores, native iOS, licensed-translation scraping, exhaustive commentary, full Bible reader.

## Non-replaceable value
Original-language exegesis + systematic "people God used" case studies + hands-free contemplative audio + AI pastoral counseling that never claims to be God's voice.
