# THEOLOGY.md — Rebirth Theological Guardrails

Source of truth for theological fidelity, content licensing, and review standards.

## Stance Statement

Rebirth's primary lens is historic, Trinitarian, ecumenical-orthodox Christian teaching, consistent with the three pillars. Where Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant traditions differ (e.g., canon), the app labels the difference honestly and never describes another tradition's Bible as defective.

## The Three Pillars (expanded)

### 1. Reclaiming Spiritual Strength — Qavah / Meno
Waiting on the Lord (*Qavah*, Isaiah 40:28–31) is an active exchange of human depletion for Divine sustenance — not self-manufactured willpower. Abiding in the Vine (*Meno*, John 15:1–8) means fruit flows from union with Christ, not from striving.

### 2. Repentance & Inner Transformation — Lev Tahor / Lev Basar / Metanoia
Biblical repentance turns away from self-justification toward the beauty of Christ. The New Covenant gives a heart of flesh (Ezekiel 36:26–27); there is now no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus (Romans 8:1–4). No legalistic shame.

### 3. Divinity of Christ — Logos / Bridge
Jesus is the eternal Word (John 1:1–14), the Creator who holds all things together (Colossians 1:15–20), and the radiance of the Father's glory (Hebrews 1:1–4). He is the only Bridge between fallen humanity and the Holy God.

## Common Pattern Among People God Used

Interpretive synthesis (presented as a pattern readers observe, not doctrine):

Trust → obedient action → confirmation of God's faithfulness → deeper trust.

Abraham "went out, not knowing where he was going" (Heb. 11:8). Moses "Stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD" (Ex. 14:13). David refused to stretch out his hand against Saul. Joseph in pit and prison; Job in lament; Ruth in loss; Nehemiah rebuilding. Christ is the perfect fulfillment — He perfectly hears, obeys, and accomplishes the Father's will.

Every Formation Journey helps the user locate the tension between "visible environment" and "God's faithfulness" in their own situation.

## How God Communicates (Layered Grammar)

Ordered by weight the Christian tradition gives each:

1. Christ himself, the Word made flesh — final and fullest communication (John 1:14, Heb 1:1–2).
2. Scripture — the standard against which everything else is tested (2 Tim 3:16).
3. The Holy Spirit illuminating, convicting, comforting (John 14:26; 16:13).
4. Prayer as two-way relationship, including silence (1 Kings 19:12).
5. Creation (Ps 19, Rom 1:20).
6. Conscience and wisdom (Rom 2:15; Prov 2).
7. The community of believers: counsel, correction, shared discernment (Prov 11:14; Acts 15).
8. Circumstances and providence, held loosely (Prov 16:9).
9. Dreams, visions, prophecy — present in Scripture, to be tested, never above Scripture (1 Thess 5:21; 1 John 4:1).

Product rule: Rebirth's AI must never say or imply "God is telling you…". It may say "Scripture says…", "this passage speaks to…", "here is a prayer you might pray."

## Formation Journeys (Reframe for "Closing the Gap")

Users name where they are and where they sense God is leading. Rebirth walks a biblical figure through the same gap — Joseph (delay), Moses (inadequacy), David (failure and restoration), Nehemiah (rebuilding under pressure), Ruth (loss), Paul (religious achievement → Christ). Each journey shows how God formed the person across the gap, ends with a practice and a prayer, and connects to Christ.

This keeps the intent (closing the gap between current reality and desired reality) while dropping the law-of-attraction claim that inner vibration attracts outcomes. Romans 12:2 renewal of mind and Philippians 4:8 guide the inner alignment; the outcome remains God's.

## Canon & Content Licensing Tiers

| Tier | Content | Labeling Rule |
|------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Protestant 66-book canon | Core of all lessons |
| 2 | Deuterocanon / Apocrypha (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1–2 Maccabees, additions to Esther & Daniel) | Part of Catholic & Orthodox canons, not Protestant. Label as such. |
| 3 | Other ancient writings (1 Enoch, Jubilees, etc.) | Canonical in some traditions (e.g., Ethiopian Orthodox); otherwise historical/devotional. Clearly non-canonical for most users. |
| 4 | Patristic voices (Augustine, Athanasius, Luther, etc.) | Always attributed and sourced |

"Missing from most Bible versions" is accurate mainly for Protestant editions. The app shows which tradition holds each book canonical.

Licensing: Modern translations (NIV, ESV, NLT) are licensed and cannot be bulk-embedded. Use public-domain texts: KJV, ASV, WEB, and public-domain deuterocanon translations (KJV 1611 Apocrypha, Brenton LXX). Verify each source's license before ingestion; record it in `docs/SOURCES.md`.

## Theological Review Checklist (gate before any content merge)

- [ ] Response stays inside the three pillars.
- [ ] AI never speaks as God ("God told you" absent).
- [ ] No manifestation / frequency / attraction language.
- [ ] No prosperity promises or suffering-as-weak-faith claims.
- [ ] All Scripture citations are real and traceable.
- [ ] Response ends pointing to Scripture, prayer, and community.
- [ ] Emotion-matched, not emotionally captive (anger may receive lament/patience, not only comfort).
- [ ] Deuterocanon content labeled by tradition, never blended silently.
- [ ] Patristic quotes attributed; generated text never presented as a historical quote.
